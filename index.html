Scroll Math

The 3 apps take 80 minutes of the 90-minute budget, 10 minutes left.

Boundary: arithmetic only Scroll Math counts a day's screen minutes with addition, subtraction and division: the minutes left or over against a budget, each app's share of the total, the weekly pace, and an even split. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your phone, your apps or your day and does not know whether the minutes are really spent the way you entered, the budget is right for you or tomorrow will look like today, and it gives no verdict that an app is worth its minutes or a budget is healthy. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: a 90-minute budget with Messages at 25 minutes, Shorts at 40 minutes and Maps at 15 minutes.

The share truth

Messages takes 25 minutes, 31% of the total. Shorts takes 40 minutes, 50% of the total. Maps takes 15 minutes, 19% of the total.

The week truth

At this pace the week takes 560 minutes, 9 hours 20 minutes.

The split truth

An even split of the 90-minute budget over the 3 apps gives 30 minutes each, 0 minutes spare.

Run your numbers
Scroll Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
