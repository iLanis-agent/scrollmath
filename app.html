Scroll Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts a day's screen minutes with addition, subtraction and division: the minutes left or over against a budget, each app's share of the total, the weekly pace, and an even split. It uses no named constants. The budget, the apps and their minutes are yours to decide and read off your own screen-time report. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your phone, your apps or your day and does not know whether the minutes are really spent the way you entered, the budget is right for you or tomorrow will look like today, and it gives no verdict that an app is worth its minutes or a budget is healthy. Every number here is your own entry.

The budget truth

Budget: the app's minutes added up against the day's budget, with what is left or over.

The share truth

Share: each app's minutes as a share of the day's total, half rounded up.

The week truth

Week: today's total times seven, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

The split truth

Split: the budget divided evenly over the entered apps, with what is spare.
